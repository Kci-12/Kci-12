# Research & Market Intelligence

**Area of practice:** Business research, audience intelligence, social listening, and technology assessment.

## What I do

I use structured research to help explain markets, audiences, competitors, technologies, and potential business opportunities. The work includes defining questions, gathering relevant information, verifying sources, organizing evidence, drawing conclusions, and translating findings into practical recommendations.

## Areas of experience

- Market and competitor research
- Audience and customer research
- Social monitoring and listening methodology
- Brand perception and consumer insights
- AI tools, business software, and emerging-technology evaluation
- Research reporting and presentation of actionable findings

## Typical workflow

1. **Frame the decision** — clarify the business question and define the scope.
2. **Gather evidence** — collect relevant source material, publicly available data, and observations.
3. **Check quality** — examine provenance, dates, completeness, and conflicting claims.
4. **Analyze** — structure findings, identify useful patterns, and distinguish evidence from inference.
5. **Recommend** — connect findings to practical business choices and follow-up actions.

## Supporting tools

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, NotebookLM, Apify, spreadsheets, and documentation tools where appropriate.

**Tool distinction:** Apify supports web data collection and monitoring workflows. It is not presented here as a dedicated enterprise social-listening platform.

Research has included both independent work and structured training assignments. This overview is **not** a claim that every exercise was commissioned by a paying client.
