# Pro Christo TV — Digital Video Production Workflow

**Published work:** [Pro Christo TV on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@PRO-CHRISTOTV)  
**Project type:** Repeatable content production, creative direction, quality assurance

## Production need

Produce coherent, polished faith-based videos from approved source scripts while maintaining consistency between narration, visual storytelling, editing, and the intended message.

## Workflow

**Approved script → narration preparation → storyboard → scene production → review and corrections → video editing → publication**

The work relies on a structured production process rather than ad hoc generation of visuals.

## My contribution

- Planned production workflows and coordinated the stages of delivery.
- Organized scripts into visual sequences and shot descriptions.
- Directed AI-assisted asset creation and continuity reviews.
- Checked scenes against approved narration and storyboards, correcting mismatches.
- Managed video editing, post-production, and final output review.
- Prepared repeatable production notes and handoff documentation.

## Tools and methods

ChatGPT, ElevenLabs, Canva, CapCut, and project-specific media workflows. The emphasis is on creative direction, production coordination, testing, and consistency—not a claim of software engineering or bespoke video-model development.
