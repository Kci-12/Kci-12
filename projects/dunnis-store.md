# Dunni's Store — E-commerce & Business Systems

**Live project:** [www.dunni.store](https://www.dunni.store)  
**Project type:** Business storefront, product administration, customer inquiry workflow

## Business need

Create a convenient way for customers to browse jewelry and wristwatches online while giving the business owner a manageable product catalogue and a route for customer enquiries.

## Solution

A responsive storefront with product and category pages, owner-managed listings and images, and WhatsApp-based enquiries for eligible products. The project also includes owner-facing analytics and reporting features to help review traffic sources and customer interest.

The website uses a **Next.js-based implementation**, with **Supabase** for database/backend services and **Vercel** for deployment.
